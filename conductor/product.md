# Initial Concept

`beancount-blue` is a collection of plugins for the [Beancount](https://beancount.github.io/docs/) plaintext accounting tool. These plugins provide additional functionality to help automate and streamline your bookkeeping.

# Vision
To provide a suite of robust, well-tested, and easy-to-use plugins for Beancount users, focusing on automation (importers), financial management (amortization, capital gains), and data integrity (tagging, clearing residual lots).

# Core Features
- **Amortization:** Spread expenses over a specified period.
- **Auto-Tagging:** Automatically add tags based on transaction patterns.
- **Capital Gains:** Calculate UK capital gains following specific tax regulations.
- **Data Cleanup:** Automatically clear residual lots in investment accounts.
- **Importers:** Support for various financial services (Monzo, Starling, TrueLayer) to automate transaction entry.

# Target Audience
- Users of the Beancount plaintext accounting system.
- Individuals looking to automate their financial tracking.
- UK-based users needing specific capital gains calculations.
- Developers looking for high-quality, typed, and tested Beancount plugins.
